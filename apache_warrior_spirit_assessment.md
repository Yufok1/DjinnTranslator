## The Apache Way: Core Principles

### The Six Pillars of Apache Warrior Spirit

1. **Earned Authority (Merit-Based Leadership)**
   - Authority comes from contribution, not title
   - Merit lies with individuals, not organizations
   - Non-transferable between projects
   - **Warrior Principle**: Leadership through proven valor in battle

2. **Community of Peers (Warrior Brotherhood)**
   - Individuals participate, not corporations
   - Flat structure - all votes equal weight
   - Benevolent Dictators For Life are **disallowed**
   - **Warrior Principle**: Band of brothers/sisters fighting together

3. **Open Communications (Transparent Battle Plans)**
   - All decisions made publicly
   - Archived mailing lists for asynchronous collaboration
   - Private decisions on code/direction **disallowed**
   - **Warrior Principle**: No secret alliances or hidden agendas

4. **Consensus Decision Making (War Council)**
   - Self-selected teams of active volunteers
   - Heavy emphasis on consensus to maintain momentum
   - Voting when consensus impossible
   - **Warrior Principle**: Democratic warrior councils making battle decisions

5. **Responsible Oversight (Code of Honor)**
   - Trust-based governance model
   - Peer-reviewed commits
   - Protection of Apache brand and community
   - **Warrior Principle**: Warriors bound by honor codes

6. **Independence & Pragmatism (Sovereign Warriors)**
   - Vendor neutrality - no organization gains special control
   - Apache License provides maximum freedom to users
   - "Community Over Code" maxim
   - **Warrior Principle**: Independent warriors serving the greater good
